What BeyondEight replaces

The tools of a studio, built for individual choreographers.

It is not just a website. It is the business layer around your work: the public page dancers see, the registration system they use, and the backend that keeps you organized.

01

Your public stage

Publish polished pages for workshops, intensives, auditions, teams, privates, merch, and announcements.

02

Your registration desk

Collect dancer details, waivers, level preferences, emergency contacts, add-ons, and custom form answers.

03

Your checkout counter

Accept deposits, full payments, payment plans, discount codes, recurring billing, refunds, and receipts.

04

Your class clipboard

Track who is registered, paid, waitlisted, checked in, grouped by level, and assigned to each session.

05

Your dancer portal

Give dancers and parents one place for updates, rehearsal files, reminders, notes, and member-only posts.

06

Your business dashboard

Understand revenue, retention, attendance, conversions, profitability, campaigns, and what to launch next.
